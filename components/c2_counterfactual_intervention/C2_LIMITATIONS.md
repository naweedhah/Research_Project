# Limits and missing evidence

- The current bundled food examples have broad group scope and household purchase quantities. They do not verify a child's serving size, nutrient intake, full diet, preparation safety or treatment.
- WHO 2023 complementary-feeding age scope applies to 6–23 months. An older-child practice rule for 24–59 months requires separate source review. Under-six-month complementary-food candidates are excluded.
- PHM feeding columns lack verified direct-observation provenance. The interface requires an explicit reported/observed choice; an unverified choice cannot become confirmed automatically.
- DCS prices represent main Colombo markets. Kalmunai prices and actual stock are not verified. Income cannot stand in for an actual food budget.
- Unknown allergies, local availability, practical capacity, individual suitability and nutrition adequacy remain unknown; explicit conflicts reject a scenario.
- A C1 output is future model risk context, not a confirmed diagnosis. No candidate is sent to C1 and no child growth benefit is inferred. Referral notice is independent of food-card ranking.
- The current evidence yields no verified eligible primary card. Showing unresolved alternatives is intentional. Professional nutrition/clinical review, local price and availability evidence, and age-specific quantitative food guidance are required before real recommendation ranking.
