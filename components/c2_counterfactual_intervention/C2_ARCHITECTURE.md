# C2 dietary counterfactual architecture (v2)

The new local research workflow is `POST /interventions/baseline-c1-v2` followed by `POST /interventions/diet-scenarios-v2`. The first endpoint calls genuine GA-RGCA-v3 once for an invented original profile. The second endpoint receives that frozen assessment and never imports or calls a predictor. Budget, allergy and reported diet changes do not trigger C1 re-prediction in the dashboard while the original model input is unchanged.

`nutrition_scenarios_v2.py` validates the confirmed or unverified baseline, keeps C1/historical/contextual information immutable, selects up to four food-based household purchase examples from eight source-priced combinations using reported food groups, budget and exclusions, compares their represented food groups with the reported baseline, applies safety and evidence checks, and separates fully verified ranks from unresolved/rejected examples. `evidence_registry_v2.py` records publication scope. `evaluation_v2.py` is the reproducible software audit. The versioned `method` selector supports bounded search, FACE-inspired food-transition search, DiCE, Wachter and `compare_all`. Candidate IDs from available methods are deduplicated before the common eligibility and ranking rules. Existing C1-sensitivity and PHM reports remain historical material and do not feed v2 rankings.

The v2 bounded baseline enumerates four selected examples from eight source-priced combinations. The v2 FACE-inspired method builds a directed graph for each example: a node is a subset of proposed foods, an edge adds one food, and an explicit exclusion blocks that edge. It returns a complete path and one unit of engineering path cost per addition. These constructed intermediate states are **not** empirical reference-density observations, and an empty food-ID start does not establish the caregiver's reported current food groups. The old `face_graph.py` numeric-feature graph and `dice_method.py` official dice-ml implementation still run in the separate synthetic software-test pipeline.

For v2, DiCE reports unavailable because no validated guideline-feasibility classifier and non-sensitive labelled reference dataset have been handed off. Wachter likewise reports unavailable for real nutrition use because no valid differentiable nutrition target has been established. `wachter_method.py` contains a genuine bounded continuous optimization over an **invented software-test target** and independently checks its discretized output against that fixture. Its objective values are not nutrition or C1 scores. Neither unavailable method silently falls back to bounded search. `compare_all` exposes all four statuses under identical v2 inputs.

When `research_algorithm_demo=true` is explicitly requested, `algorithm_demo_v2.py` runs official dice-ml on a fully enumerated, labelled one-hot fixture and a separate bounded SciPy continuous optimization on the same engineering target. That target is exactly one catalogue option with indicative cost no higher than the submitted budget and no explicitly excluded or reported-unavailable food. It is **not** a nutrition-suitability or C1 outcome target. All returned IDs are checked against the original deterministic target and then pass through the unchanged v2 evidence checks. The response exposes a separate `software_test_ranked` list, ordered by fewer listed purchase items, reference cost and stable ID. `ranked_cards` remains empty when real eligibility evidence is missing. A lower-cost basket or shorter shopping list is not a prediction of improved child health.

Algorithm foundations: [DiCE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07697), [FACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09369), and [Wachter et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00399). Those are algorithm references, separate from the food guidance in `C2_GUIDELINE_EVIDENCE.md`.

Feature classes: food choice/group coverage and confirmed feeding pattern are actionable scenario inputs; budget, income, prices, availability, allergies and preparation resources are contextual; birth and breastfeeding records are historical; age, sex, original anthropometrics and the original C1 assessment are immutable. Household income is never treated as a food budget. The fixed shopping quantities are household pricing examples, not child servings or treatment diets.

The old `PHM_C1_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` result (3 model-rule passes among 2,837 candidate C1 comparisons) describes the earlier methodology. It is neither overwritten nor used as a nutrition-ranking performance estimate. The new workflow compares reported diet with alternative foods, not clinical predictions.
