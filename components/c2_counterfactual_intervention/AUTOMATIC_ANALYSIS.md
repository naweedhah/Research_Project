# Automatic C1 research comparison

`GET /interventions/automatic-baseline?case_id=synthetic_24m` loads the actual GA-RGCA-v3 prediction for a fixed, invented input. `POST /interventions/automatic-analysis` accepts a case ID (`synthetic_5m`, `synthetic_24m`, or `synthetic_59m`), optional food budget in LKR, and `daily`, `weekly`, or `monthly` budget period. The 5-month case does not call C1: its required meal-frequency input would be age-inapplicable, so the response contains no prediction or feeding candidates.

For older cases, bounded search uses the existing adjacent-category policy, deduplicates transitions, and re-scores every candidate through `RealC1HttpPredictor` with `allow_demo=false`. The adapter verifies the loaded model, provenance, output semantics, feeding categories, and unchanged historical input snapshot. Any increase in predicted severity, underweight or stunting probability, or MAM or SAM probability rejects the candidate from exploratory ranking. Remaining candidates are ranked by number of changed feeding features, then categorical change key. A rank means only an ordering of unresolved model comparisons; it is **not** an intervention rank or clinical benefit score. The response always returns zero approved cards until separate authorization and evidence exist.

DiCE and FACE-inspired graph execute separately on the same invented input through the existing synthetic method facade, with an unapproved research policy and no food-planning assertions. Their result summaries are marked `SIMULATED_TEST_ONLY` and never merge with C1 predictions. These methods have no real C1 model/reference-data adapter.

The official Sri Lankan Nutrition Division [resources index](https://nutrition.health.gov.lk/english/resources/) lists the [practitioner's handbook](https://nutrition.health.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/FBDG-Practitioners-Handbook-Final-English.pdf). Its contents identify FBDG 2 (cereals), FBDG 3 (vegetables, leafy vegetables and fruits), FBDG 4 (fish, eggs and pulses), FBDG 16 (complementary feeding after six months), and FBDG 17 (children and adolescents). [FAO's profile of the Ministry-endorsed guidelines](https://www.fao.org/nutrition/education/dietary-guidelines/regions/sri-lanka/en/) independently lists these broad food groups and says the guideline messages include children under five. `guideline_references.py` stores source, section, broad age scope and published-scope review status. Its 24–59-month label is an age grouping for this research display; it does not assert age-specific portions. The [Family Health Bureau 2025 caregiver booklet](https://fhb.health.gov.lk/resources/2025-booklet-on-complementary-feeding-for-caregivers-e-en) is listed, but its detailed pages have not been reviewed for this workflow. No individual serving quantity or model-category intervention rule has been approved.

The DCS October 2026 source-checked records supply published Colombo District *unit prices*. `educational_baskets.py` maps the source-listed groups to checked commodities and builds four illustrative household shopping purchases. Quantities are explicit pricing assumptions, **not child servings**, daily feeding amounts, complete diets, or treatment. Daily mass quantities are one seventh of the weekly example; pieces and bunches are rounded up to whole purchases. Monthly examples are four weekly purchases. Decimal cost calculations compare only matching budget periods. Missing prices produce unknown fit; historical observations remain labelled historical. A fixed sort puts within-budget examples first, then above-budget, then unknown; each group orders by represented food-group count, price evidence, item count, indicative cost and title. This shopping order never changes C1 predictions or intervention approval. `food_feasibility.assess_food_alternative` continues to guard real intervention proposals. Kalmunai prices and availability, individual suitability, caregiver practicality, programme eligibility, and real-mode policy authorization remain unknown.
